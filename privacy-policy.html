Quick Summary

  • ReliefRadar shows your location on a map to help you find and rate nearby public restrooms — that location is never sold, and it never leaves your device except to power the map and nearby-search features you're using.
  • There are no accounts, logins, emails, or phone numbers required to use the app. If you choose to join the public leaderboard, you can pick an optional nickname — the only name-like information ReliefRadar ever collects, and only if you opt in.

  • We don't run analytics or tracking software, and we don't sell your data to anyone.
  • The app may show a small, non-intrusive banner ad (no video ads) to keep ReliefRadar free — see the Advertising section for details.

1. Introduction

ReliefRadar ("we," "us," or "the app") helps people find and rate public restrooms nearby — in businesses, parks, transit stations, and other public places — so no one has to search blindly in a moment of need. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

2. Information We Collect

Location data

With your permission, ReliefRadar uses your device's location (via Apple's Core Location and MapKit frameworks) to center the map on your area, find nearby restrooms, and let you place a pin at a location when adding a new restroom. Location is requested only while the app is in use — ReliefRadar does not request or use background/"always" location access. You can allow, deny, or later revoke this permission at any time in your device's Settings.

Restroom ratings and descriptions you contribute

When you rate a restroom or select descriptive tags (like "Clean," "Free to Use," or "Handicap Accessible"), that information is stored on our backend (Google Firebase/Firestore) and shown to other users. Restroom names come either from Apple's Maps place data or from a reverse-geocoded street address for locations not tied to a recognized business — we do not accept freely-typed names, so this data isn't a place for personal information to end up.

Optional leaderboard nickname

ReliefRadar includes an optional public leaderboard that ranks contributors by the number of unique restrooms they've rated. If you choose to participate, you can pick a nickname (2–20 characters: letters, numbers, and underscores). That nickname, along with your restroom-rating count, is stored on our backend and displayed publicly to anyone using the leaderboard. This is entirely optional — every other feature of the app works without ever choosing a nickname — and the nickname isn't verified against, or linked to, any real-world identity. Please don't choose a nickname that reveals your real name or other personal information if you'd rather keep it private.

Basic technical data

Our backend infrastructure (Firebase) automatically logs basic technical information common to any app request, such as approximate timestamps of database activity. This is standard infrastructure logging, not used to build a profile of you.

3. Information We Don't Collect

  • No account creation, sign-in, email address, or phone number is required or collected to use the app. Choosing a public leaderboard nickname is entirely optional — see "Optional leaderboard nickname" above for what that involves.
  • No photos, camera, contacts, or microphone access.
  • No advertising identifiers or analytics tracking at this time.
  • No precise identity is attached to the ratings or tags you submit — contributions are anonymous.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information above only to:

  • Show your position and nearby restrooms on the map.
  • Let you add a new restroom entry at a specific location.
  • Display community ratings, tags, and (where available) live business hours from Apple Maps.
  • Keep the restroom directory accurate by preventing duplicate entries for the same real-world location.

5. Third-Party Services

ReliefRadar relies on a small number of third-party services to function:

  • Apple Maps & MapKit — powers the map display, place search, and business hours lookups. Apple's handling of this data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase (Firestore) — stores restroom locations, ratings, and tags. Google's handling of this data is governed by Firebase's Privacy and Security policy.

6. Advertising

ReliefRadar is, and will always be, completely free with no premium subscriptions or in-app purchases. To help cover the small ongoing cost of running the app, ReliefRadar may display a small banner advertisement provided by a third-party advertising network such as Google AdMob. We intentionally keep this to a single, non-intrusive banner — we do not use video ads, interstitial (full-screen) ads, or pop-ups, and we don't intend to.

Ad networks may collect device-level information (such as an advertising identifier or general device data) to deliver and measure ads. Where required, the app will ask for your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking-based advertising occurs. We will update this section with the specific ad provider's own privacy policy once advertising is active in the app.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Restroom ratings and tags — and, if you've chosen one, your leaderboard nickname and rating count — are shared publicly within the app by design, since the entire point is to help other users. Location and technical data are shared only with the service providers described above, solely to operate the app's core features.

8. Data Retention

Restroom entries, ratings, and tags are retained indefinitely to keep the directory useful for the community, since they aren't tied to an individual identity. A claimed leaderboard nickname is retained indefinitely as well, for as long as the leaderboard exists. Location data used to center the map or place a pin is not stored beyond what's needed to complete that action.

9. Your Choices

  • Location access: control or revoke it anytime in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Location Services > ReliefRadar.
  • Contributed content: because ratings and tags aren't linked to an account or identity, we're unable to identify or delete a specific person's past submissions on request. If you believe specific content should be removed (for example, if it's inaccurate or was submitted in error), contact us below and we'll review it.
  • Leaderboard nickname: the app doesn't currently offer a way to change or remove a claimed nickname yourself. If you'd like yours removed, contact us below and we'll take care of it manually.

10. Children's Privacy

ReliefRadar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us so we can address it.

11. Security

We use industry-standard practices provided by Apple and Google's infrastructure (including encrypted data transit) to help protect information handled by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to use reasonable means to protect it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves — for example, when advertising becomes active. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
